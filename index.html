01 · What we're about

One happy home, three big ideas.

Everything we do at Katie's comes back to three simple things. They shape our rooms, our routines and the way we care for every child.

01 · Play

Play freely.

Messy, noisy, imaginative play is how children make sense of the world, so we give them lots of time for it, indoors and out.

02 · Learn

Learn gently.

Stories, songs, counting and making things. We follow each child's interests and help them build confidence one small step at a time.

03 · Grow

Grow together.

Sharing, kindness and friendship. We keep in close touch with families, so home and nursery work together for every child.

03 · A day at Katie's

Busy days, happy faces.

Every day is a little different, but a familiar rhythm helps children feel safe and settled. Here's what a typical day looks like.

  1. Morning

    Hello & welcome

    A warm welcome at the door and a quick catch-up with parents before the fun begins.

  2. Mid-morning

    Play & discover

    Free play, messy play, building, painting and small-group activities, with a healthy morning snack.

  3. Late morning

    Fresh air

    Outdoor time for running, digging, splashing and spotting minibeasts.

  4. Lunchtime

    Lunch & rest

    Lunchtime together, then quiet time and naps for those who need them.

  5. Afternoon

    Stories & songs

    Circle time, music, stories and an afternoon snack before home time.

      07 · FAQs

      Good questions.

      The things parents ask us most. If your question isn't here, just get in touch.

      How do I book a visit?

      Send us a message using the form below, or give us a call. We'll arrange a time for you and your child to come and look around and meet the team.

      What ages do you care for?

      We care for children from birth to 4 years old, across our three rooms: Tiny Treasures (0–2), Little Gems (2–3) and Shining Stars (3–4).

      How does settling in work?

      We offer free settling-in sessions so your child can get to know their key person and room before they start.

      Are snacks included?

      Yes. A healthy morning snack and afternoon snack are included in our hourly rate.

      Is there a minimum booking?

      Yes, the minimum booking is 6 hours per day. We're open from 07:30 to 17:30, and funded hours can be used at any time across the day.

      What should my child bring?

      A change of clothes, a coat suitable for the weather, and a comforter if they have one.

      How will I know how my child's day went?

      You'll get a daily update on meals, naps, nappies and activities, plus regular progress updates from your child's key person.
